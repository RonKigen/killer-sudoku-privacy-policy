Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Thank you for choosing to play our Killer Sudoku puzzle game ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding any data associated with your gameplay experience. This policy outlines how we handle information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

Our app is built to function seamlessly offline and locally on your device. Depending on your interactions, we may process:

  • Gameplay Data & Cages Progress: Data related to your puzzle completion states, active grid progress, daily challenge trophies, mistakes count, and best times stored locally.
  • User Profile & Preferences: Optional profile names, custom avatars, theme selections, and sound settings.
  • Analytics & Diagnostics (Optional): Standard anonymized performance metrics or crash diagnostics to ensure app stability across different devices.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Save and restore your active Killer Sudoku cage matrices and game states.
  • Track personal achievements, streaks, and statistical records.
  • Improve performance, fix bugs, and optimize the game engine.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your game sessions, puzzle progression, and statistics are stored securely on your local device. If optional cloud backup features or account login synchronizations are utilized, your information is encrypted in transit and at rest.

4. Third-Party Services

We may integrate trusted third-party SDKs (such as app distribution services or anonymous crash analytics tools) that collect standard diagnostic information in compliance with their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Killer Sudoku game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any such data has been provided, please contact us for immediate removal.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may modify this privacy policy from time to time. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy or the Killer Sudoku app, please reach out to our support team.
